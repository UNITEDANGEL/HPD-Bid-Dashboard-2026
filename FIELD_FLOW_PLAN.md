# HPD Field Flow Plan

Updated: 2026-07-21

## Job Card Flow

The job card must be fast in the field and should not require hunting through long sections.

1. Header: OMO, address, Waze, Google, Map.
2. Scope Preview: show the important ITB page 3 scope as a short readable preview. Do not use a nested scrollbar. Full scope opens through the ITB page/detail action.
3. Source Program: show HPD/AEP/ERP source clearly in a compact card.
4. Tenant Contact: show name, phone, apt, call/text/WhatsApp in a compact card when available.
5. What Happened: status selection must appear immediately after the field facts. This is the main field action.
6. Status Date: user can use now, pick date/time, or clear status.
7. Media: only opens after status/start work choice. Before/after media buttons are compact and labelled.
8. Package: affidavit/invoice/package review appears after the job is closed or ready.
9. Utilities: map layers, archive map, and clear status are secondary compact controls, never large cards.

## Scroll Rule

The selected job card must have one main scroll surface. No nested scrolling inside scope or button sections.

## Performance Rule

Avoid heavy blur, large shadows, and oversized button stacks inside the open job card. Primary actions can glow; secondary actions stay compact.
