United Angel Construction HPD Jobs

Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026

United Angel Construction Corp operates this tool for its work-order administration. For privacy questions or requests, contact uac525@gmail.com.

Information accessed

When authorized by the Google account holder, the importer uses Gmail read-only access to search for HPD work-order correspondence and retrieve messages and attachments. Imported records can include work-order numbers, addresses, award and maturity dates, work descriptions, award amounts, and tenant contact details included in those documents. The app also records information entered by its operator, such as notes, job outcomes, media and paperwork.

Purpose and processing

Imported information is used to organize work orders, display job locations, support fieldwork and prepare documents. Job addresses may be sent to the configured geocoding service to obtain map coordinates. Opening external navigation sends the chosen destination to the navigation provider. Gmail read-only authorization does not permit this importer to send, modify or delete messages.

Storage and access

Downloaded documents and extracted records are stored in the operator's project files. Dashboard data may be copied to the configured hosting service and project repository for deployment. Project folders may also be synchronized by the operator's storage services. Some field information is saved in browser storage or server files. These copies are controlled by the operator and the access settings of the services used. Avoid entering information unnecessary for the work order.

Use and disclosure

The purpose of Google data access is this work-order workflow, not advertising or sale of mailbox data. Hosting, storage, mapping and document-delivery services may process the information required for their functions. The operator can export or share job documents as part of the work-order review and submission process.

Disconnecting and deletion

You can revoke the app's access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not delete previously imported files, browser records, deployed copies or backups. Contact the operator at the address above to request correction or deletion of those records. Retention depends on the operator's work-order recordkeeping requirements and the copies maintained in its systems.

Updates

This page will be updated when the described data practices change.
