United Angel Construction HPD Jobs

A work-order management tool operated by United Angel Construction Corp.

The app helps the contractor organize NYC HPD awards and invitations to bid, locate work orders on a map, record field activity, and prepare invoices and affidavits for review.

Google connection

With the account holder's authorization, the importer uses Gmail read-only access to find work-order emails and download supporting attachments. That connection does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

This contractor-operated tool is not an official NYC HPD application.
